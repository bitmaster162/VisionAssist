# VisionAssist Gated Delivery Queue

This file is an evidence-gated queue, not a feature wishlist.

## Now - P1 Intent and Human-AI Fusion Proof

Benchmark ID:
`VISIONASSIST_CHART_INTENT_AND_HUMAN_AI_FUSION_PROOF`

```text
60 market cases + 15 non-market visual cases
  -> human prior freeze
  -> AI-only assessment freeze
  -> human+AI revision freeze
  -> outcome reveal
  -> blinded scorecard
```

### Prepared

- deterministic 75-slot roster,
- R29 lineage and portfolio contract,
- executable artifact contracts,
- outcome commitment and freeze/reveal hash chain,
- Brier, accuracy, quality, abstention, leakage, and correction scorer,
- candlestick comparator boundary,
- synthetic lifecycle and metric tests,
- proof-pack checklist,
- pre-registered 60-case market sampling commitment,
- 60 real market chart cases frozen at `CASE_FROZEN`,
- 45 development and 15 blinded-holdout market receipts,
- pre-registered 15-case synthetic visual-control commitment,
- 15 synthetic visual-control cases frozen at `CASE_FROZEN`,
- 10 development and 5 blinded-holdout visual receipts,
- complete 75-case roster with 55 development and 20 holdout slots,
- fail-closed human-prior draft/submission tooling,
- preselected five-case blind process batch with external role-binding template,
- exact R33-to-R29 handoff binding,
- verified local custody tree commitment for ignored cases and vault bytes.

### Blocked

- separated curator/custodian/analyst/adjudicator assignments,
- signed external bindings from real people to frozen role IDs,
- Git `HEAD/tree` identity or explicitly authorized genesis commit,
- controlled custody transfer or independent custodian evidence,
- frozen human, AI, fusion, and baseline annotations,
- blinded outcome reveal and independent adjudication.

The synthetic visual-control slice closes the current roster but does not prove
natural-scene generality. Its geometry QA is `USABLE_WITH_LIMITATION`: 12 cases
show all six generated trail points; three have edge clipping and retain four
or five visible points. Frozen evidence remains immutable.

### Exit gate

P1 exits only when all 75 receipt chains verify, all 20 holdout cases remain
blind through fusion freeze, the scorecard gates pass, and an independent
review accepts the evidence. Passing synthetic tests only proves harness
behavior.
